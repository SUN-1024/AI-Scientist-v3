# Goal
Develop and evaluate anatomy-preserving patient-level machine unlearning for medical image segmentation. When a patient requests removal, approximate a model trained from scratch without that patient's data while preserving segmentation utility on other patients and reducing update cost. Do not equate inaccurate predictions on forgotten patients with successful unlearning.

This first AI-Scientist v3 run is an ACDC development pilot, not the final publication experiment. Produce executable evidence and a clearly labeled pilot manuscript. A positive result advances to a larger ACDC/KiTS23 confirmatory study; a negative or inconclusive result must be preserved. Do not claim first medical segmentation unlearning, certified removal, legal compliance, clinical safety, or publication acceptance.

# Scientific question and competing explanations
Hypothesis: preserving retained anatomy, especially boundary structure, during selective forgetting gives a better deletion/utility trade-off than generic gradient ascent or retain-only fine-tuning.
Alternative 1: apparent forgetting is simply global model damage. Prediction: forgotten-patient changes accompany comparable heldout utility losses and differ from clean Retrain.
Alternative 2: apparent benefit is additional retain training or extra compute. Prediction: equal-time retain-only fine-tuning matches the proposed method.
Alternative 3: output agreement hides residual membership information. The pilot cannot exclude this; independent patient-level attacks are a REQUIRED later publication gate, not a result to infer from Dice or output distance.
Candidate exploration may use retain-boundary consistency, gradient conflict handling, or parameter-selective updates. These are hypotheses, not established novelty. Implement ONE fixed candidate per attempt; do not modify evaluator, splits, score, labels, architecture, or clean-retrain reference.

# Evaluator-owned metrics and aggregation
Compute foreground Dice for RV, myocardium, LV per patient by pooling all that patient's slices, then average classes, patients, and seeds equally. A GT-absent class is excluded from that patient's utility macro, but report absence counts; a present class with empty prediction has Dice=0. Evaluate all forget patients and all heldout patients, with labels kept private during prediction.
Compute mean absolute probability difference from clean Retrain on forget patients, in [0,1]. To prevent background dominance, first average within GT foreground versus background, then average these two regions equally when both exist; then average patients equally. Only evaluator computes this distance; candidate receives neither labels for prediction nor reference outputs.
Compute 1-pixel-tolerance foreground boundary F1 per patient on 2D slices with scipy morphology, aggregating counts over slices before class/patient averaging. Label it explicitly pixel-space pilot boundary F1. Do not call this native-volume surface Dice or HD95.
Search combined_score = 0.5*(1-forget_retrain_probability_mae) + 0.3*heldout_macro_dice + 0.2*heldout_boundary_f1. This is a screening score, not privacy certification. Declare finite scalar metrics for all three components, forget Dice, Original/Retrain utility, reference distance for baselines, and trusted measured elapsed times. A missing/failed seed contributes combined_score=0 and explicit failure evidence, with fixed equal seed weights; do not omit cases.
Scientific validity requires correct patient/split handling, real data and training, isolated candidate execution, valid finite normalized predictions, unchanged evaluator, completed required comparisons, and reliable output receipts. Search eligibility does NOT require beating baselines. Final claim assessment is separate: if Original or Retrain heldout Dice<0.50, call the pilot undertrained/inconclusive; never claim successful unlearning. Any candidate utility/distance trade-off must be shown against EVERY required comparison. Beating the scalar score alone is not sufficient for a method claim.
Required controls: Original is the no-update control. An all-background prediction control must score zero on foreground Dice for a patient with foreground. A deterministic non-identity patient-label permutation control on at least two shape-compatible heldout cases must be recorded as a diagnostic; no guaranteed degradation claim if anatomy is similar. Reject an identity permutation. Controls are evaluator-owned and need no extra candidate reset. Do not use candidate flags or state hashes as proof of forgetting.
